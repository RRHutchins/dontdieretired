Three rules we live by

  1. Start smaller than feels worthwhile. Ten minutes of walking beats a plan you never begin. Every person we write about started with something embarrassingly small.
  2. Do it with people. Nearly every story here involves a club, a class, a coach or a friend. Company is the strongest predictor we've seen of people still doing the thing a year later.
  3. Check with your GP, then get on with it. Ask what you can do, not only what you can't. The answer is usually "more than you think".
