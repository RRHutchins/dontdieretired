    Email my list to myself

    Your list is kept on this device, in this browser only. We never see it, and there is no account. A backup is a small file you keep: restoring it adds to what is here and never wipes anything. "Email my list" opens your own email app; the address never reaches us. On an iPhone or iPad, Safari can clear a website's saved data if you don't visit for a while, and the app on your home screen keeps a separate copy from the website, so a backup is worth having.

      How it works

      • Every link was checked by us. Each challenge links to the official event, governing body or club finder, opened and read on the date shown. We don't list dates or prices that go out of date: the official page has those.
      • It's free. The list, the suggestions and the tracking cost nothing.
      • Do it with someone. Every challenge has an invite link you can send to a friend. For company beyond that, the clubs and groups in each challenge are the place to start{% if cfg.social.facebook %}, and you can tell us how you get on at our Facebook page{% endif %}.
      • Something missing? Add it to your own list, and tell us: we add new challenges as we verify them.
