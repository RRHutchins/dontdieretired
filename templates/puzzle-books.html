Something to do between the pudding and the walk: 105 puzzles of seventeen kinds, nearly all with something of Christmas or midwinter in them.

  • Three parts. Gentle for the armchair, Steady when you want more, and Tricky for the quiet hour when everyone else has gone for a walk.
  • Large print. Every puzzle and its instructions are in 16-point type or larger, with room to write.
  • One answer each. Every logic puzzle was checked by computer to have exactly one answer, so there is always a way through without guessing. The solutions are at the back.
  • A big page. A paperback of 146 pages, 8.5 × 11 inches (21.6 × 27.9 cm).

What's inside

Some will be old friends and some you may never have met. The front of the book explains how each one works.

  • Word search
  • Cryptograms (including lines from Dickens, in code)
  • Anagrams
  • Missing vowels
  • Word ladders
  • Logic puzzles (who brought the mince pies?)
  • Nonograms (pictures hidden in a grid)
  • Sudoku
  • Killer sudoku
  • Kakuro
  • Futoshiki
  • Calcudoku
  • Skyscrapers
  • Binary puzzles
  • Bridges
  • Trees and presents
  • Star battle

How it was made: the puzzles come from our own program and a second, separate program re-checks every one. The book's words and pictures were made with AI and checked by us, and Amazon was told that when it was published.
