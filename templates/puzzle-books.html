New for Christmas 2026

The Don't Die Retired Christmas Quiz Book

Three nights of family quizzing: 300 questions in 30 rounds, ten for Christmas Eve, ten for Christmas Day and ten for Boxing Day.

  • For the whole table. Every round has questions marked for anyone, for grown-ups, and a couple of hard ones. Each night has a junior round and a swap round, where grown-ups answer about the children's world and the children about the grown-ups'.
  • Made for the quizmaster. One person reads aloud. Each round starts on a left-hand page so the whole round is in view, in large type, and its foot gives the page of its answers.
  • Every answer checked. Each answer was confirmed on a page from a museum, library, official body or other good reference, and many come with the story behind them.
  • Two editions. Most rounds are in both. The UK edition adds rounds such as Kings and queens and Panto and the stage; the American edition has Presidents and The fifty states. A paperback of 116 pages, 6 × 9 inches.
