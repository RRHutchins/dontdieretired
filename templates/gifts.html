Questions

How do I give an experience as a present?

Book a date, or buy a gift voucher from the operator so they choose the day. Either way, print or write a card that says what it is and where, and add something small from the kit list to unwrap.

Will it arrive in time{% if season %} for Christmas{% endif %}?

We do not sell anything ourselves: every link goes to the shop or operator, and they set the last order and delivery dates. Amazon shows its delivery date before you pay. Experiences need no delivery, so they work as late presents.

Where do the reviews come from?

Where we show a top pick, an also-recommended and a lower-cost pick, they are named products from an independent reviewer's published review, with their label and their own words, and a link to read the whole review. We do not rank or test products ourselves.

Do you earn from these links?

Some links are affiliate links (Amazon, Viator, GetYourGuide): if you buy through them we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. It never decides what goes on these pages.
