What Planner Plus is

Planner Plus is the planner on Don't Die Retired. You choose the things you want to do this year, from {{ list_total_text|lower }} things on Things to do, or ideas of your own, and give each one a day or a month. For anything from Things to do, Planner Plus writes a dated plan: when to book, what to do to get ready and when, and the reason each step comes when it does. All your plans then sit together in My year, on a calendar, and in the calendar app you already use.

Why you would use it

Most things worth doing need a little organizing before the day: a course to book before it fills, a festival whose dates move each year, a trip with travel and a bed to sort out, an event that needs weeks of training. Planner Plus does that working-out for you and puts each step on a date, so you can see at a glance what comes next, and the year fills up with things to look forward to.

What it does

  • A dated plan for anything on Things to do: a course, a festival, a day out, a trip, an event or a new habit. Pick a day or a month and the plan shows when to book and each step to get ready, with the reason it comes when it does. Ideas of your own go in My year too, on their date, without a get-ready plan.
  • Training for physical events: for the events on our list that have one, a week-by-week training plan worked back from the day, at the starting point you choose, as part of the plan rather than the whole of it. It is general guidance, put together from published sources, and has not yet been checked by a coach, physiotherapist or doctor.
  • Your trip, with the day itself: where it is on a map, journey planners for getting there, hotel searches for your dates, hostels and campsites, a map search for places to eat nearby, and other things on our list in the same area that you can add to the same trip. These are searches you run yourself: we do not book anything, and we have not checked or chosen the places they find.
  • My year: all your plans on a month calendar and month by month, with each step to tick off as you go. It notes when two plans fall on the same day or two training plans overlap, and moves a flexible step off a busy day, saying why.
  • In your own calendar: add one address to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook and My year keeps itself up to date there. You can also save a calendar file at any time.
  • On every device: sign in with your email address (there is no password) and your list and plans are the same on your phone and your computer.

Good to know

  • Plans are general information to help you get organized, not medical, training or travel advice. Check with your GP (family doctor) before starting something much harder than you are used to, and check dates and booking details on the organizer's own site.
  • Some links, such as tour searches and some booking pages, are affiliate links: if you book through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. They are marked where they appear, and never go into your calendar.
  • Your account holds your email address, your list and plans, whether you are a member and, if you make one, your calendar address; the privacy page has the details.
