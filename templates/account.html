My year in your own calendar

Add My year to the calendar on your phone or computer once, and it keeps itself up to date: new plans, changed dates and steps you tick off on any device show up there by themselves. Your calendar app decides how often it looks again, so a change can take some hours to appear.

Add to Apple Calendar

  • iPhone, iPad or Mac: press Add to Apple Calendar, then Subscribe.
  • Google Calendar: on a computer, open Google Calendar; beside "Other calendars" choose +, then "From URL", and paste the address. It then shows on your phone too.
  • Outlook: choose Add calendar, then Subscribe from web, and paste the address.

Keep this address to yourself: anyone who has it can see your plans (but not your email address or anything else about your account). It is shown only now. If you lose it, or share it by mistake, make a new one: the old one stops working at once.

Making a new address, or stopping this one, stops the calendar you already added: it gets no more changes, and you would add the new address to your calendar again.
