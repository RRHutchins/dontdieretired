£20, including VAT, for twelve months of Planner Plus.

  • It does not renew, so nobody is charged again.
  • The twelve months start when the code is used, not when you buy it, and the code does not run out.
  • You pay on Stripe's secure page; we never see your card.

Buy a gift What Planner Plus does

How it works

  1. Sign in on your account with your email address. There is no password, and an account is free.
  2. Buy the gift under "Give Planner Plus". Once Stripe confirms the payment, usually within a minute, the code appears on your account.
  3. Print the card. Add the names if you like (they are printed on the card only and never sent to us), then print it or save it as a PDF. You can see and print the code again from your account until it is used.
  4. They use the code at dontdieretired.com/redeem: they sign in with their own email address and type the code. Their twelve months start then.

Your account shows whether each code has been used, and when, but not by whom.

What they get

A dated plan for anything on Things to do, with the steps to get ready, a training plan for physical events that have one, getting there, where to stay and eat, and more to do nearby; all their plans together in My year, on every device and in their own calendar. See a sample plan.

Questions

What if they are already a member?
If their Planner Plus is set to end, the gift's twelve months follow on from it. If they have a subscription that renews, they stop the renewal first (from their account), then use the code, so they never pay twice.
What happens after the twelve months?
Plus stops; nobody is charged. In the last weeks, their account page offers them the chance to carry on, and a subscription starts only if they choose one. Their plans stay on their account either way.
Can I get my money back?
If the code has not been used, yes: ask us ({{ contact() }}) within 30 days of buying it and we refund the £20 in full; the code then stops working. If it has been used, you can still ask within 14 days of buying it, and their twelve months then end; after that it cannot be refunded. See the terms.
Can they choose a different email address from mine?
Yes. The code is not tied to anyone until it is used, and nothing from your account is shown to them.
