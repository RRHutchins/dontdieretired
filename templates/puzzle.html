    How it works

    • Four different puzzles a day, the same for everyone, changing at your own midnight.
    • Three levels. Gentle, Steady and Hard are separate puzzles, not the same one with fewer hints, so you can do the Gentle ladder and the Hard sudoku on the same day. Change level whenever you like.
    • The Wheel: each letter can be used only as often as it appears. No names and no hyphens. British and American spellings both count. The harder the level, the more words you need.
    • The Ladder: any real word will do for a rung, as long as only one letter changes each time.
    • Sudoku: every row, column and outlined box holds each number once. Gentle is a small six-by-six grid. Every grid has exactly one answer.
    • Numbers: add, subtract, multiply or divide. Every answer along the way must be a whole number above zero. Every target can be reached.

    Hints are free and never cost you your streak.
