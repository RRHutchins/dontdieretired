On January 15, 1885, Wilson Bentley made the first known photograph of a single snowflake, seen through a microscope.

He lived on a farm in Jericho, Vermont. He had been given a microscope for his fifteenth birthday, and he combined it with a camera to photograph the tiny ice crystals.

Over the rest of his life he photographed more than 5,000 snow crystals. His neighbors nicknamed him Snowflake. He sold many of his glass plates, the pictures he made on glass, to schools and colleges for 5 cents apiece.

His pictures supported the belief that no two snowflakes are alike.

Will you look closely at the next snow that falls?
