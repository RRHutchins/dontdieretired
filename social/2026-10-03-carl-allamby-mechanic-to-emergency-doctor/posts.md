He fixed cars in Cleveland for 25 years. One compulsory biology class later, he was on the way to the emergency room.

Carl Allamby opened a car repair shop in East Cleveland at 19 and enrolled in night classes for a business degree at 34. A required biology course revived a childhood wish to be a doctor. He graduated from Northeast Ohio Medical University in 2019 at 47 and joined Cleveland Clinic's Hillcrest Hospital as an attending emergency physician in 2022, at 51. How he did it, what the research says about how adults learn, and how to start.
