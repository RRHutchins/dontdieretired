Medicare covers a one-time "Welcome to Medicare" preventive visit in your first 12 months of Part B, and then a yearly "Wellness" visit once every 12 months. You pay nothing for either if your provider accepts assignment, says Medicare.gov.

The catch: neither is a routine physical exam, which Original Medicare doesn't cover. And if extra tests or services are done in the same visit, you may owe coinsurance or the Part B deductible.

Many screenings and shots also cost nothing with a provider who accepts assignment, including screening mammograms, colorectal cancer screenings, flu shots and cardiovascular screening blood tests, each with its own rules on how often and who qualifies.

Our guide sets out what each visit includes, the main screenings with how often Medicare covers them, and the questions to ask when you book. Have you had your Wellness visit this year?
