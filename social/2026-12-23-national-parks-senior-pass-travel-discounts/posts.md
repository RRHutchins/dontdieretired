The America the Beautiful Senior Pass costs $80 and lasts for life, says the National Park Service. US citizens and residents aged 62 or older qualify, and it covers entrance fees at national parks and other federal recreation sites.

Buy it in person at a federal site, or order it from the USGS Store, which adds a $10 processing fee. A $20 Senior Annual Pass is also sold, and annual passes can be traded in toward the lifetime one.

Other discounts are real too, but smaller: Amtrak takes 10% off most rail fares for travelers 65 and over, and several states have their own park passes or discounts.
