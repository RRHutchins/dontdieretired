A former miner built a dome from a rainwater tank. Now his name is on a paper about a shape never seen on Saturn.

In September 2026 the journal Science Advances described a decagon, a ten-sided atmospheric wave, around Saturn's south pole. One of its authors is Trevor Barry, an amateur astronomer who left school after Year 10 and spent 30 years in the mines of Broken Hill. How a homemade observatory ended up in a scientific paper, what the evidence says about looking up, and how to find Saturn this month.
