Jeremy Lockwood retired as a GP, started a PhD and found three new dinosaurs in boxes nobody had looked at properly.

Dr Jeremy Lockwood, 68, a retired GP from the Isle of Wight, took up a PhD in palaeontology at the University of Portsmouth after retiring. Studying thousands of bones held at Dinosaur Isle and the Natural History Museum, he has identified three new dinosaur species, most recently the sail-backed Istiorachis macarthurae, described in August 2025.
