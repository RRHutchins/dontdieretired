In a free class at the Indianapolis Public Library, the teacher gave students one simple job. Check that what the AI points to actually exists.

The teacher is Janna Kreider. The class uses AI chatbots such as ChatGPT or Google Gemini on library laptops. Students try everyday tasks, such as grocery lists and meal plans.

Pam Parks had used ChatGPT only a couple of times before a class. Her sister had encouraged her to try it.

Mike Biro already used ChatGPT to tailor his résumé for each job. Mirror Indy, a local newsroom, quotes him on the class: "It's really opening me up to different things."

Dan Snodgrass, another tech learning specialist at the library, shared a tip on AI video. If a clip is 10 seconds or under, look at it with more skepticism. AI tools are really good at making those short clips.
