Miss your first chance to sign up for Medicare Part B (medical insurance) and you can pay a late penalty every month for as long as you have it. The exception is if a current job's group health plan covers you.

Medicare is the US government's health insurance program. Our plain guide explains what Parts A to D cover, and the two main routes: Original Medicare with Medigap (extra insurance from private companies that fills the gaps), or a Medicare Advantage plan (a private plan that bundles the parts together).

It also lists every enrollment window and the 2026 and 2027 costs from CMS, the agency that runs Medicare, and where to get free, unbiased help.
