Some volunteer roles in the US pay a small hourly stipend, a modest allowance rather than a wage. It goes to volunteers on lower incomes, in two of the three programs run by AmeriCorps Seniors, a US government volunteering service.

Foster Grandparents mentor children and young people. Senior Companions keep neighbors company and help with errands, so they can stay in their own homes. Both serve at least five hours a week.

The amount varies by local project. Multnomah County, in the US state of Oregon, lists $4 an hour (about £3) for its Foster Grandparents. It says this does not affect benefits such as SNAP, the US food assistance program.

The third program, RSVP, lets you choose your own kind of service, from a few hours a week. If none of the three fits, Idealist, a volunteer search site that now includes VolunteerMatch, lists more than 100,000 other roles.

Our guide puts the three side by side, with questions to ask a local project. Which would you try?
