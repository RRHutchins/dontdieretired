Byron Bay to Steep Point: 6,300 km, three deserts, no support vehicle, 44 days. He says he does it for fun.

Craig Johnston, 56, of Gymea Bay in Sydney, left Byron Bay on 23 July 2026 and rode about 6,300 km (3,900 miles) to Steep Point in Western Australia in 44 days and 10 hours, sleeping in a tent or the occasional pub. That took more than 16 days off the record for the unsupported crossing. How he did it, what the research says about time on a bike, and how to build towards a multi-day ride of your own.

Carry what you need, ride what you can, sleep where you stop. The distance looks after itself.
