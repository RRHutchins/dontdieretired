Eight times over, a Hyrox race asks for the same thing: run a kilometer, then do something heavy. In the standard men's race that means pushing a sled loaded to 152 kg (335 lb), pulling one of 103 kg (227 lb), burpee broad jumps for 80 m, a 1,000 m row, lunges with a 20 kg (44 lb) sandbag and, at the end, 100 throws of a 6 kg (13 lb) ball at a target on the wall.

At the 2026 Hyrox World Championships, Kevin Marshall, of Launceston, and Danny Wood, of Truro, took it on together and won the men's 60 to 64 age group as a pair. The Cornish & Devon Post reported in July that the two Cornishmen had beaten some of the world's top athletes in a sport that is one of the fastest growing in fitness.

> "Standing on the top of the podium with the national anthem playing was an unbelievable experience."
> — Kevin Marshall, to the Cornish & Devon Post

## Saturday mornings in Lifton

The pair trained at the Tamar Performance Centre in Lifton, with the coaches Matt Luxton and Charlie Wevill, over months of preparation. Marshall thanked the athletes who come to the center's Saturday sessions for their support.

Luxton was clear about who the champions are. "They're both local men with jobs, families and everyday commitments," he told the paper. "This isn't really a story about elite athletes."

What pleased the coaches as much as the medals, he said, was seeing people who had raced at the World Championships "training alongside complete beginners every week".

## Two people, one race

In Hyrox doubles, partners run every kilometer together and split each station between them however they choose. That makes the partnership part of the tactics: who takes the sled, who takes the wall balls, and how to share the row.

Wood said that Wevill had paired him with Marshall and that the two complemented each other well. He also said Wevill's coaching was unlike anything he had met before. "He quickly identified the areas where I needed to improve and helped me work on those weaknesses," Wood said. Wevill, for his part, spoke of steady improvement built on consistency and hard work, and just as much of friendship and community.

## Why this matters

Hyrox is a neat fit with what the health services ask of adults. The CDC (the US public health agency) says adults need at least 150 minutes of moderate activity a week and two days of muscle-strengthening activity. The NHS (the UK health service) gives the same 150 minutes, or 75 of vigorous activity, and asks for strengthening work on at least two days that covers the legs, hips, back, abdomen, chest, shoulders and arms.

Most people find the running easier to fit in than the strength work. A Hyrox race, and the training for it, does both: half the race is running, and the other half is pushing, pulling, carrying and lunging. Our guide to [strength training](/move/strength-training-after-50-why-and-how/) explains why that second half is worth the effort whether or not you ever race.

## How to start

Hyrox describes itself as open to everyone, from professional athletes to people who simply want a goal for their training. Doubles halves the work at each station, and relays split the race four ways. Our [Hyrox page](/list/do-a-hyrox/) has the full format, the weights for each division and UK and US events. If you have not exercised for some time or have a medical condition, check with a GP (family doctor) or your doctor first.

For a long build on foot, Steve James's [marathon a day around Britain](/stories/steve-james-200-marathons-coastline/) shows the same habit at work: steady training, with people around you.
