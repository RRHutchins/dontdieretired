# AI section review, November 2026

Run on Monday 2 November 2026 (RUNBOOK §6b and §6e). Sources were re-read by a helper session; edits were then made by the daily run. Scope: the pillar and the six guides in `category: ai` dated 2026-10-02 (plus the Margaret Colling story, which cites no maker or reporting pages), and `content/scam_help.yaml`.

## How pages were read

Same method as October. Pages were read with the fetch tool only, which gives a small model's extract of the page in answer to a question, not the raw page. "Unchanged" means the extract confirmed the guide's wording, setting name, number or period. The fetch tool would only open a URL after it had appeared in a web search result in the same session. Some URLs were refused before any request was sent ("blocked": the permission request timed out). Others failed at the site ("client_error"). Both are marked "could not fetch". Pages were fetched one at a time.
