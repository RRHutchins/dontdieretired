## Presents for people who would rather do something

We have put together a gift section: **[presents for people who love doing things](https://dontdieretired.com/gifts/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=gifts)**. It is sorted by what the person loves doing, from walkers, swimmers and stargazers to makers, cooks and the one who keeps saying they'll learn the piano.

Each idea comes with the ways to give it:

- **Book it for them:** a class, a day out or a trip, with where to search and book, and what to check first.
- **Give the kit:** where an independent reviewer has named their picks, we show their top pick, another they recommend and a lower-cost one, in their words, with a link to the full review.
- **Give your company:** some of the best ideas cost nothing, so the present is a promise to go along.

A few places to start:

- [Presents for walkers and hikers](https://dontdieretired.com/gifts/walkers/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=gifts): a guided day up Ben Nevis, a national trail, a foraging walk.
- [Craft and art classes](https://dontdieretired.com/gifts/makers/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=gifts): a pottery wheel class, a glassblowing taster, a silver ring they make themselves.
- [Adventure experiences](https://dontdieretired.com/gifts/adventure/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=gifts): a balloon flight, a trial gliding lesson, the flying trapeze.
