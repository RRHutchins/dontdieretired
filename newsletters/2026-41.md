## This week's lead

**[Bored of his usual route, Nigel Beecroft ran every street in Birmingham instead](https://dontdieretired.com/move/nigel-beecroft-every-street-birmingham/)**

Nigel Beecroft was tired of running the same loop from his home in Harborne. So he began ticking off the rest of Birmingham, cul-de-sacs included. About two years and 2,500 miles later he ran the last of roughly 8,000 streets, and went for a pint.

**Try this week.** Print or screenshot a map of the streets within half a mile of your front door. Walk one you have never been down, and colour it in when you get home.

**Or, if you're already fit:** pick an area with 150 to 200 streets and finish all of it in eight weeks, running or walking briskly four times a week.
