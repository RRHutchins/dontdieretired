## This week's lead

**[Ron Wallace grew the world's heaviest pumpkin in his first season under glass](https://dontdieretired.com/eat/ron-wallace-heaviest-pumpkin/)**

The scale at a Rhode Island weigh-off settled at 3,156lb (1,431.5kg), nearly 300lb more than the world record Ian Paton had set in England seven days earlier. Every giant starts as one seed in a small pot in April, and the ground for it can be got ready this month.

**Try this week.** Choose the sunniest, most sheltered spot you have and spread garden compost at least 5cm (2in) thick over it. The RHS says you can plant straight into it in spring.

**Or, if you already grow:** go for an 'Atlantic Giant' next season. When the fruits reach 6 to 8 inches across, keep the largest and remove the rest.
