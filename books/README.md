# Don't Die Retired puzzle books

Print-on-demand puzzle books for Amazon KDP, generated by program.

    pip install ortools reportlab wordfreq english-words pillow
    python books/build_book.py vol1 hard xmas     # interiors and covers into books/out/
    python books/verify.py vol1 hard xmas         # independent check of every puzzle; exit code 0 means all passed

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `puzzles.py` | One generator per puzzle type. Every logic puzzle is proved by a solver to have exactly one solution before it is used. |
| `layout.py` | Draws each type, as a puzzle or as its solution. |
| `content.py` | Themed word lists, cryptogram lines (proverbs and out-of-copyright works only), instructions. |
| `build_book.py` | The books (`BOOKS`), page layout, wraparound cover sized to the page count. |
| `verify.py` | Re-reads a built book and re-checks every puzzle with separate code. Run it before every upload. |

Format: 8.5 x 11 in paperback, black ink on white paper, no interior bleed; cover has 0.125 in bleed and a clear
panel for the barcode. Builds are seeded, so the same command gives the same book.

## Making the next book
1. Add an entry to `BOOKS` in `build_book.py` with a new `seed`, `volume`, `file` and `mix` (how many of each type per part).
2. A new book must be genuinely different from the earlier ones (Amazon removes near-duplicates): change the mix,
   the difficulty, the themes in `content.py`, or make it a single-type book.
3. Pictures for nonograms are hand-picked in `PICTURES` (puzzles.py): render a symbol at the grid size, look at it,
   and add it only if it is recognisable. Do not reuse a picture within a book.
4. Word ladders use everyday words only. After building, read the ladder solutions; add anything crude, obscure or
   a name to `_BLOCK` and rebuild.
5. Build, run `verify.py`, and look through the rendered pages before sending the files to Robin.

`books/out/` is not committed: this repository is public and the PDFs are for sale.

## State on 6 October 2026
Three books: `vol1` (Volume 1, 136 pages), `hard` (The Hard Ones, 156 pages), `xmas` (Christmas Edition, 146 pages,
seventeen kinds including Trees and presents, Star battle and Missing vowels).

- **Large print.** Everything a solver reads on a puzzle page, and the instructions, is 16 point or larger. Solutions
  are four to a page and smaller; the copyright page says so. Keep to this in new books and new puzzle kinds.
- **Kakuro** uses a repair method (see `kakuro` in puzzles.py), which makes 8, 9 and 10-square grids with one answer.
- **Logic puzzles** are only accepted if their clues fit the page at 16 point above the answer table.
- **Author and copyright holder.** Robin decided on 6 Oct 2026 to publish as "Don't Die Retired": that is the author
  name on KDP and the name in `COPYRIGHT_LINE` (build_book.py). Changing it does not change page counts.
- The closing page must not promise anything the site is not doing (no weekly email or daily puzzle claims).

## Quiz books (started 24 October 2026)
Robin asked for a Christmas quiz book in two editions, UK and US, of 300 questions each, for family quiz nights:
30 rounds of ten, read aloud by a quizmaster, answers at the back. `build_quiz.py uk|us [--sample]` builds a
6 x 9 in interior from `quiz/rounds.yaml`; questions and answers are 16 point, rounds start on a left-hand page
so the whole round is in view, and each round's foot gives the page of its answers.

- **Rounds.** 20 shared rounds (`editions: [uk, us]`) and 10 for each edition. Planned shared rounds: Christmas
  around the world (done), Christmas food and drink, carols and Christmas music (facts only, never lyrics after
  1928), Christmas at the cinema, winter wildlife, snow ice and weather, the night sky, inventions, famous firsts,
  explorers, words and where they come from, numbers, animals, the human body, food around the world, art,
  classical music, books and authors, world sport, countries and capitals. UK: a very British Christmas (done),
  kings and queens, British history, around the UK, British inventions, Christmas telly and radio, UK sport,
  Christmas number ones, famous Britons, panto and the stage. US: an American Christmas, presidents, American
  history, the fifty states, American inventions, holiday films and TV, US sport, American music, national parks
  and landmarks, famous Americans.
- **Audience (Robin, 24 Oct 2026).** A family quiz for all ages, sold as a stocking filler to (or for) whoever hosts
  Christmas, and played round the table from grandparents to grandchildren. So every round has three questions a child
  of 8 to 12 could answer (`level: 1`, printed as "anyone"), five for grown-ups (`level: 2`) and two hard ones
  (`level: 3`). Prefer questions with a story in them: a child should enjoy hearing the answer even if they did not
  know it. Each night (ten rounds) also has one **junior round** (pitched for children, shared by both editions) and
  one **swap round** (grown-ups answer about the children's world, children about the grown-ups'; per edition, and
  nothing that will date before Christmas or that needs a source we cannot open). That makes each edition 3 junior
  + 3 swap + 17 shared general + 7 edition-only rounds.
- **Checking.** Every answer is confirmed on a page opened that day (museum, national library, official body,
  learned society, well-edited reference; Wikipedia only to find sources), recorded in `source` with the date in
  `checked`. Questions without `checked` are not printed. Notes add nothing the source does not say. Where
  accounts differ, the question avoids the disputed point or the note says so. Mix in each round: about four easy,
  four medium, two hard.
- **Not yet built:** cover, score sheet, the three "night" title pages in a full build, a checker for duplicate
  answers across rounds. Amazon may treat two editions sharing two-thirds of their questions as near-duplicates:
  keep titles, covers and descriptions clearly marked UK and American, and watch for a KDP query.
