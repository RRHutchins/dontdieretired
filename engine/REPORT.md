## Disagreements and how they were resolved

- **strength-twice-weekly**: Higdon's beginner marathon plan suggests people new to weights wait. The official guidance and the injury evidence carry more weight, so plans include strength work, starting light with body weight.
- **drink-to-thirst**: British Cycling's beginner plan suggests sipping every 10 to 15 minutes on a ride. The international consensus statement outranks it, so plans say drink to thirst and the cycling statement on fuel does not set a drinking schedule.
